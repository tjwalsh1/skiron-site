Privacy

Last updated October 4, 2026

Who runs this site

Skiron is run by Thomas Walsh in Iowa City, Iowa. For questions about this page or your information, email:

What the site collects on its own

Nothing. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising tools. Its fonts, scripts and images all come from its own address, so your browser does not contact other companies when you read it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives ordinary web server information, such as your IP address, when you visit. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you send a message

The contact form asks for your name, your email address, an optional organization and your message. We use them to read and reply to your message. We do not sell them or use them for advertising. Thomas keeps messages as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the conversation.

The form is run by Web3Forms. It passes your message to Thomas by email and keeps a copy for up to three years. Web3Forms operates from India and uses Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Hetzner to run its service. It may send your IP address and email address to anti-spam services, CleanTalk and Akismet, to check for abuse. Read the Web3Forms privacy policy. If you would rather not use the form, you can email Thomas directly.

Your choices

You can ask what Thomas holds about you, ask for a correction, or ask for your message to be deleted, by emailing that address. Thomas will act on reasonable requests, including for the copy held by the form service where possible.

Do Not Track

This site does not track visitors, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off.

Children

This site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top will change with it.
